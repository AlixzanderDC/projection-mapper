Workflow & keys
  1. Add a projector, open its window, drag that window onto the projector's display and press F.
  2. Add a surface and pick a source. Drag its corners onto the real object (in this preview or straight on the projector).
  3. Curved object? Raise the grid to 3–7 columns/rows and pull the inner points.
  4. Camera tab: start the back camera, Calibrate (auto) while the camera sees the projected area, then Trace object and click its four corners. Handles can be dragged on the camera image too.
  5. Two projectors on one wall? Give each a surface with a crop (Split into 2), overlap them, and set the edge blend on the touching edges.

Space play/pause · Arrows nudge (Shift = 10px) · Tab next point · G source/grid/solid · Esc deselect
